### Q5 (en) — PASS
**Q:** What factors determine affiliate commissions?

**Expected:** They vary by product category, country, campaign, margin and affiliate type

**Answer:** Affiliate commissions at Latram Shop are not a single flat rate; they are calculated based on several variables that the program defines for each affiliate and each promotion. The key factors are:

- **Product category** – Different categories may carry different commission percentages. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  
- **Country** – The geographic market of the sale influences the rate. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  
- **Specific campaign** – Campaign‑level rules can set unique percentages or bonuses. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  
- **Margin** – Higher‑margin items may allow a larger commission share. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  
- **Affiliate type** – Latram Shop can assign distinct rates for different affiliate tiers or partners. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  

In addition to these primary determinants, the final commission amount is adjusted according to the **outcome of the sale**, including:

- Sales that are fully validated versus those that are cancelled, returned, or refunded. (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  
- Any operational adjustments required by the internal policy (e.g., partial refunds). (Programa de Afiliados, p. 3)  

All of these elements are defined in the Affiliate Program documentation and may be combined differently for each campaign or affiliate relationship.
